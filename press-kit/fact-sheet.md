# Analog Click — Fact Sheet

**Analog Click: Haptic Switches** turns the iPhone screen into a panel of machined metal controls: sliders that snap to a magnet, rollers that ratchet under your thumb, reels that keep spinning after a flick, a rotary dial that whirs back, an ignition key, and a throttle grip. Every movement pairs a sound synthesized on the device with a Core Haptics pattern, so it feels like handling a real object.

## Highlights

- Clicks are generated in real time, vary slightly on every strike, and come from where the control sits on screen.
- The motorcycle exhaust, cassette deck, and bicycle freewheel sounds are continuous generators driven by how fast you click.
- The barometer reads the iPhone's pressure sensor on the device to show the coming weather, and tonight's moon with a tap.
- Controls can also play music, run the flashlight, start a focus timer, and adjust volume and brightness.
- The free version includes the music controls and the large ring reel.
